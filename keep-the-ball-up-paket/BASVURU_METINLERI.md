# Keep the Ball Up — CrazyGames başvuru metinleri

Form İngilizce doldurulur; metinleri olduğu gibi yapıştırabilirsin. Başvuru gönderilmedi.

## Title
```
Keep the Ball Up
```

## Description
```
Keep the ball in the air for as long as you can.

Run under the ball and the kid does the rest: a header when it lands on top of him, a shoulder or a knee a little to the side, a foot further out. Jump for high balls, slide for far ones. Friends and a dog drop in to pass with you, a hoop appears for a quick basket, and big objects like a cannon or a slide throw the ball back to you.

A short hands-on tutorial at the start shows how to run, jump and slide. Every 90 points the page turns into a new place: the street, the beach, a train, the clouds, the moon and many more. You have six balls. Drop one and you lose 10 points; every 200 points gives a ball back. Beat your best score!

Everything is drawn by hand on paper.
```

## Controls
```
A / D or arrow keys - move
W or up arrow - jump
Space - slide to reach a far ball
M - sound on / off

Phones: slide your thumb on the left to run, jump and slide buttons on the right
```
